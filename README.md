\# Power BI Semantic Modeling



\*\*From Business Meaning to Reliable Decisions\*\*



A collection of practical guidelines and articles on semantic modeling, metadata, and governance in Power BI.



The goal is to connect business meaning with technical implementation and support consistent, transparent, and reliable data-driven decisions.



\## Documentation



\### Semantic Modeling Guideline



A practical guideline covering semantic model design, star schemas, relationships, DAX measures, metadata, governance, quality assurance, and documentation.



\*\*Version:\*\* 0.1 (Draft), November 7, 2026

\## Core Principle



\*\*Meaning first. Technology second.\*\*



Semantic modeling starts with business decisions, responsibilities, and definitions, not with the available source tables.



\## Status



The guideline is currently a draft. Its rule classifications are recommendations and do not represent formally approved organizational standards.

# Power BI Semantic Modeling

**From Business Meaning to Reliable Decisions**

Practical guidelines and insights on semantic modeling, metadata, and data governance in Microsoft Power BI.

This repository explores how well-designed semantic models connect business requirements with technical implementation, creating a reliable foundation for consistent analytics and informed decision-making.

It is for Power BI semantic modelers and BI teams, as well as business stakeholders who define or validate metrics and reporting needs.

The focus is on a simple principle:

**Meaning first. Technology second.**

**Current guideline status:** Version 0.1 (Draft), last updated November 16, 2026.

## Start here

- **Designing or reviewing a semantic model?** Start with the [Semantic Modeling Guideline](docs/guidelines/Guideline_Semantic_Modelling_Power_BI.pdf) (English) or [German version](docs/guidelines/Guideline_Semantische_Modellierung_Power_BI.pdf).
- **Clarifying a decision, dashboard need, KPI, or model documentation?** Browse the [Practical Resources & Templates](docs/ressources/README.md) overview to find the appropriate canvas, template, or checklist.


## Documentation

### Semantic Modeling Guideline for Power BI

A practical guideline for designing, developing, documenting, and maintaining reliable Power BI semantic models.

**Topics covered:**
- Semantic modeling principles and business definitions
- Star schemas, fact tables, and dimensions
- Relationships, granularity, and aggregation
- DAX measures and time intelligence
- Naming conventions and usability
- Metadata, documentation, and data lineage
- Performance, security, and governance
- Quality assurance and review processes

**Available languages:**

- [Deutsch | Guideline herunterladen](docs/guidelines/Guideline_Semantische_Modellierung_Power_BI.pdf)
- [English | Download Guideline](docs/guidelines/Guideline_Semantic_Modelling_Power_BI.pdf)

### Articles

These articles explain why more data and AI do not automatically lead to better decisions. They show how semantic models, metadata, and governance create a shared understanding of business data, helping BI and AI deliver consistent, trustworthy insights.

## Explore the resources

The [Practical Resources & Templates](docs/ressources/README.md) overview explains how these materials fit together and helps you choose where to start. Browse the resource folders for practical canvases, documentation templates, and review tools that complement the guideline:

| Resource folder | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| [Decision design](docs/ressources/01_decision_design/) | The Decision Mapping Canvas for clarifying which business decision needs support, who owns it, and what action may follow. |
| [Requirements & stakeholders](docs/ressources/02_requirements_stakeholder/) | The Dashboard Canvas for capturing dashboard users, business questions, KPIs, data needs, and responsibilities. |
| [Semantic modelling](docs/ressources/03_semantic_modelling/) | KPI definition and semantic model specification templates, plus table and measure documentation and a metadata description guide. See the [folder overview](docs/ressources/03_semantic_modelling/README.md). |
| [Validation](docs/ressources/04_validation/) | The Semantic Model Review Checklist for recording model quality checks, evidence, findings, exceptions, and release readiness. |

The resource overviews describe each tool in more detail and link to available English and German versions.

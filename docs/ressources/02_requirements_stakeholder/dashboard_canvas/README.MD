# Dashboard Canvas

**Turn business questions into clear dashboard requirements.**

The **Dashboard Canvas** is a structured template for defining the purpose, users, decision needs, measures, analytical perspectives, data requirements, and responsibilities of a **specific dashboard** before designing its visuals.

Its guiding principle is:

> **Clarify value and questions first. Define metrics and visualizations second.**

## Purpose

Dashboards should address a genuine business need, not simply display available data. The canvas creates a common basis for discussions between stakeholders, business owners, BI analysts, and developers.

It helps establish **what the dashboard is for, what is out of scope, and what its users need to understand or decide**.

## The ten content areas

| Area | What to clarify |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Target audience / users** | Who will use the dashboard, who uses it regularly, and who is the primary audience? |
| **2. Problem, purpose, and non-purpose** | Which problem should it solve, which question should it address, and what is explicitly out of scope? |
| **3. Business questions** | Which questions, deviations, patterns, or possible causes must users be able to explore? |
| **4. Decision / action** | What needs to be decided, which information supports that decision, and who acts on it? |
| **5. KPIs / measures** | Which measures are relevant, which targets or thresholds exist, and how do the measures relate? |
| **6. Dimensions / analysis paths** | Which dimensions, filters, drilldowns, comparisons, and time perspectives are required? |
| **7. Success criteria** | How will the usefulness of the dashboard be recognized, and what improvement is expected? |
| **8. Data sources / data quality** | Where does the data originate, how reliable is it, and which gaps or limitations are known? |
| **9. Data freshness / usage** | How frequently is the dashboard used and refreshed, and in what business context? |
| **10. Responsibilities** | Who owns the business need, data, measures, maintenance, and development? |

## When to use it

- Before creating a new dashboard or significantly redesigning an existing one.
- During requirements workshops with business stakeholders.
- When a report has accumulated visuals but lacks a clear purpose or agreed audience.
- When teams need to resolve scope, KPI, data, or ownership questions before implementation.

## Suggested workflow

1. Establish the **problem, purpose, and non-purpose**.
2. Identify the **users and the business questions** they need answered.
3. Clarify the **decisions and actions** the dashboard is expected to support.
4. Define relevant **KPIs, dimensions, comparisons, and time perspectives**.
5. Identify available **data sources, quality constraints, and freshness requirements**.
6. Agree on **responsibilities and success criteria**.
7. Review open assumptions with business and technical stakeholders **before** designing visualizations or implementing measures.

For decisions that require deeper analysis of owners, timing, action options, trigger metrics, signals, and risks, use the [Decision Mapping Canvas](../../01_decision_design/decision_mapping_canvas/README.md) as a complementary tool. You do not need to duplicate every answer in both templates.

## Expected output

An agreed, dashboard-specific **requirements overview** covering users, scope, business questions, decision needs, KPIs, analytical interactions, data dependencies, operational expectations, success criteria, and ownership.

The canvas does **not** replace a detailed KPI definition, data contract, semantic model specification, or the testing and approval of the finished dashboard.

## Relationship to the other resources

| Resource | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [**Decision Mapping Canvas**](../../01_decision_design/decision_mapping_canvas/README.md) | Explores **which decision should be improved**, by whom, when, and through what action. |
| **Dashboard Canvas** | Defines **what a specific dashboard needs to provide** to support its intended users. |
| **Semantic Modeling Guideline** | Guides the **consistent and reliable implementation** of business meaning, measures, relationships, and metadata in Power BI. |

The two canvases can be used separately or together. A reusable semantic model may support multiple dashboards, so **one dashboard canvas should not be treated as the specification for an entire shared semantic model**.
